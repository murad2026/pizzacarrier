# Pizza Carrier — "Wrong / Right" video brief

**For:** the person producing the video in Google AI (Gemini / Flow / Veo).
**Client:** Pizza Carrier (carrierforpizza.com), a carry bag for pizza boxes, sold to pizzerias in the US.
**Contact:** Murad Esenov, 3724119@gmail.com

---

## 1. Goal
Show in one glance why a pizzeria should use Pizza Carrier: with the bag, a customer carries up to **4 pizza boxes in one hand**, the boxes stay **level**, and the other hand is **free** to open a door or a car.
The video plays at the top of the website and opens from a QR code on a printed flyer. Most viewers watch on a phone, often **with the sound off**, so the story must work without words or sound.

Also send: the source clips (without text) and the final still images, so labels can be changed later.

## 3. The product (get this right; it is the whole point)
- A **flat, clear, semi-transparent white plastic strip** (thin HDPE film), about 8" wide.
- It runs **under the stack of boxes, up two opposite sides, and gathers into one handle** on top, held in one hand.
- It is **not** a closed bag, not a box, not a bag with walls. The boxes are fully visible.
- Boxes: **plain brown kraft pizza boxes, no print**, square, about 12"–14". **Always 4 boxes** in the "right" shots.
- The stack stays **perfectly level**: no tilt, no sliding, in every frame.
- Reference images (attached):
  - `bag-reference-4-boxes.jpg`, `bag-reference-2-boxes.jpg`: real product photos (cropped, no faces). Use **only** for the shape of the bag.
  - `worker-with-bag.jpg`: an approved AI image where the bag looks right.
  - `right-door-clip.mp4`: an approved clip for scene 2 (keep it or redo it in the same style).

## 4. Character and location (same in every scene)
- **Man in his 30s**, short dark hair, friendly. Navy casual jacket, dark grey t-shirt, dark jeans, brown leather shoes. Reference: `character.jpg`. Keep the face and clothes identical in all scenes.
- **Pizzeria:** small, warm, brick wall, wood-fired oven, glass entrance door with a vertical metal handle. Daytime, warm light. The pizzeria and the door must be **the same** in scenes 1 and 2.
- **Parking lot:** right outside, daytime, an ordinary dark sedan. The same car in scenes 3 and 4.
- Lighting stays **daytime** across all scenes (no jump to evening).

## 5. Storyline (full version, about 16 s)

| Scene | Time | What happens | On-screen label (added in edit) |

| 1. WRONG, door | 0:00–0:03 | He holds a stack of 4 boxes in front of him **with both hands**, no bag. He reaches the glass door and tries to push it with his elbow and shoulder. The boxes wobble; he pauses, annoyed but not angry. | red ✕ **WRONG** |
| 2. RIGHT, door | 0:03–0:06 | Same man, same door. He carries the 4 boxes in the clear bag **in one hand**; the boxes are level. With his free hand he opens the door easily and walks out, smiling. | green ✓ **RIGHT** |

**Short version (10 s):** scenes 1, 2 and 5 (about 3 + 3 + 4 s).

**Tone:** light and friendly. "Wrong" scenes are mildly inconvenient, never a disaster: no dropped pizza, no slapstick.

## 7. Edit
- Labels, check mark ✓ (green #2E9E5B), cross ✕ (red #D93025), logo and website: **add in the editor, never in the AI generation.**
- Brand colors: navy **#2A498C**, orange **#E0622F**, white. Text font: bold wide sans (e.g. Archivo Expanded / Mundial Black).
- Logo: will be supplied (current temporary logo: `logo.svg`).
- Music: light, upbeat, royalty-free (e.g. from the editor's library). The video must also work muted.
- Cut each scene to its best 3 seconds. Hard cuts between WRONG and RIGHT.

## 8. Do NOT
- Do not use real people's faces or the real pizzerias from the reference photos. They are references for the bag only.
- No brand names, logos or printed text on boxes, clothes, signs or the car.
- Do not show the bag as a closed bag, a box, or with walls.
- No more or fewer than 4 boxes in the "right" scenes; no tilting boxes in "right" scenes.
- No text generated by the AI inside the picture.
- No famous music.

## 9. Checklist before delivery

- [ ] Bag matches the product: clear strip, one handle, boxes visible
- [ ] 4 boxes, level, in every "right" frame
- [ ] Free hand clearly opens the door / car in 2 and 4
- [ ] Labels, logo and website readable on a phone
- [ ] Versions A and B (and C if possible) exported at 1080p
