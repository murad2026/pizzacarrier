Pizza boxes are hard to carry.

One box or four, takeout pizza is awkward to carry. Stacked boxes tilt and slide, the pizzas shift inside, and your customer needs both hands just to hold them.

With Pizza Carrier, the boxes stay level and secure, and the whole order goes in one hand. The other hand stays free for doors and the car.

You did the work right in the kitchen. Pizza Carrier keeps it that way after it leaves the counter.

Set up in 9 seconds.

Precision-cut corner slots hold the boxes securely. No need to level the boxes: just place them diagonally.

  1. 1

    Spread the Pizza Carrier on the table.

  2. 2

    Place the boxes diagonally,

    so their corners sit in the corner slots.

  3. 3

    Fold the handles.

    Up to 4 boxes, ready to carry in one hand.

Built for pizza boxes

Specially designed for carrying pizza boxes, Pizza Carrier bags feature precision-cut corner slots that hold boxes securely. The smart design keeps boxes perfectly leveled.

Pizza Carrier bags also fit cake and pastry boxes: any square or round box of a suitable size can be packed in the bag. For pizzerias and pastry shops, we can print your logo on the bags for orders of 60 cases or more.

Frequently asked questions

How many boxes can one bag hold?

Up to 4 boxes for sizes from 10" to 15", with a total load of up to 11 lb. See the box-size table for the maximum per size.

Can you print our logo on the bags?

Yes. Logo printing is available for orders of 60 cases or more. Contact us for details.

What box sizes does it fit?

Square pizza boxes from 10" × 10" to 18" × 18", plus cake and pastry boxes of a suitable size. Larger boxes mean fewer per bag: 3 for 16" boxes and 1 for 18" boxes.

How long does it take staff to set up?

About 9 seconds: spread the bag on the table, place the boxes diagonally, fold the handles.

What is the Free Test Drive?

How does ordering and shipping work?

Carriers come 300 per case at $35 per case. Shipping is $5.99 per order, and we ship to all US states within 48 hours. After you send the form, we’ll contact you to confirm your order and payment.
